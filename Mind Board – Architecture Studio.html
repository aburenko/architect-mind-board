Sticky notes

The board never ends: drag the empty background to move around, scroll or pinch to zoom (or use the buttons bottom left). Double-click for a new note. To connect two notes, drag from the round handle on a note’s right edge onto another note; click a line to delete it. Hover a note for its drag handle, color dot, hierarchy and delete button.

Categories

Every note set to the hierarchy level Heading is automatically a category, named after its text. Open any other note to tick the categories it belongs to.

Each main category becomes a column and its subheadings become labelled groups inside it. A note in several categories appears as a copy in each of them (copies vanish with Undo sort); notes without a category go last. Notes at the Title level belong to no category; they sit once above all columns.
